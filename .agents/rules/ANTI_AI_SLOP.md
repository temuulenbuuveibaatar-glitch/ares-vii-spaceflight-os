# Anti-AI Slop Rules (Mandatory & Always Active)

Every change, layout, and copy in this project must adhere to the following rules:

1. **NO Purple-to-Blue Gradients**: Never use indigo/violet/purple-to-blue or neon glowing borders.
2. **NO Pill Buttons (`rounded-full`)**: Always use geometric container-proportional radii (`rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl`).
3. **NO Eyebrow Tags Above Headlines**: Remove small uppercase tracking tags floating directly above section titles (e.g. `// OPERATIONAL STAKEHOLDERS`, `// GAP ANALYSIS`). Let headings speak clearly for themselves.
4. **NO Random Decorative Numbering**: Never tag items with decorative `01`, `02`, `03` in tiny tracked uppercase just to make cards look "designed".
5. **NO Emoji Overload in Headings or Buttons**: Remove functional emojis (`🚀`, `💡`, `✨`, `⚡`, `🌻`) from headings and buttons. Use clean Lucide SVG icons instead.
6. **NO Em Dashes (`—`)**: Never use em dashes. Write direct sentences separated by commas, colons, or periods.
7. **NO Empty AI Metaphors**: Ban phrases like "a rich tapestry of", "at the intersection of", "a multifaceted approach", "testament to", "delve into".
8. **NO "Not Only X, But Also Y"**: State facts directly without hedge formulas.
9. **Bigger, Readable Typography**: Never hide critical content in `text-[10px]` or `text-xs`. Core body text must be `text-base` or `text-sm font-semibold` minimum.
10. **Concentric Border Hierarchy**: Respect Emil Kowalski Rule 14 (inner radius = outer radius - padding).
